Simulator 2: community quality of life and acceptance of digital technologies

Exploratory scenario model with two stocks: acceptance of digital technologies by residents (Bass diffusion, slowed by tourism pressure) and quality of life (life satisfaction, 1–10), coupled with the agritourism capacity of Model 1.
