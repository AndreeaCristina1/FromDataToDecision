Simulator 1: hotel and agritourism accommodation capacity, Gorj County

System dynamics model with three stocks (hotel capacity, agritourism capacity, reputation), a Bass reputation loop (R1), a saturation brake (B1), substitution through the allocation of total demand and a public-policy channel. Change the parameters and compare the simulation with the observed INS data for 2001–2025.
